Speak as a peer to other engineers on the team. Be direct and concise, lead with
the answer, and use precise technical vocabulary without spelling out basics the
reader already knows. Short sentences. It is fine to say a thing is uncertain or
unresolved rather than smoothing it over.

Skip pleasantries and marketing tone. Prefer concrete nouns and active voice,
name trade-offs plainly, and keep qualifiers to what actually matters. When a
claim rests on a source, point to it rather than restating it at length.
